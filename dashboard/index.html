TOKEN LAUNCH RADAR

Protocols that record no token yet, ranked by what a launch would be worth — and how to farm each one before it happens.

How the value model works

  1. Peer multiples by category. Every protocol that already trades is grouped by category; its median market-cap-to-TVL multiple, shrunk toward the global median, prices a pre-token protocol of the same kind.
  2. Size curves, global and per category. A log-log regression of market cap on TVL over all tokenised peers gives a size-only estimate; categories with 8+ peers also get their own regression, shrunk toward the global line. The implied value blends the estimates 50/50, or 40/30/30 when a category curve exists.
  3. Momentum and track record. Seven-day TVL growth moves the estimate by up to ±25%, and each year a protocol has run with no token adds 6%, capped at four years.
  4. A confidence score on every row. Peer count, fit quality and the distance of a protocol's TVL from its category peers combine into a 0–1 score with a High / Medium / Low tier, so thin-evidence estimates are labelled as such.

Methodology. The radar reads one sealed DefiLlama snapshot and fits its model on the snapshot's own tokenised protocols: an ordinary-least-squares line in log10 space (log10 market cap against log10 TVL), per category the median market-cap-to-TVL multiple shrunk toward the global median with weight n/(n+6), and — for categories with at least 8 tokenised peers — the category's own log-log line shrunk toward the global line with weight n/(n+8). A pre-token protocol's base value blends the size curve and its category multiple 50/50 in log space, or 40/30/30 with the category curve when one exists; seven-day TVL momentum then scales it by 1 + 0.01 × the change clamped to ±25%, and its track record scales it by 1 + 6% per year tracked, capped at four years, with protocols younger than a quarter year discounted 10% instead. The stated range is the estimate times 10±0.45. Each row's confidence score combines its category's peer count (40%), the R2 of the curve that priced it (35%) and the log-distance of its TVL from its category peers' median TVL (25%); tiers are High at 0.55+, Medium at 0.38+, Low below. The board covers the snapshot's full 40-protocol watchlist and fills the remaining rows with the largest pre-token protocols below the $50M floor that pass the watchlist's own hygiene: no bridge, liquid-staking, restaking or exchange categories, no wrapped or staked products, no versioned pool names, no exchange-named products. Farm plans are generated per protocol from its own chain list ordered by gas cost, its category's product mechanics and its TVL tier; costs are estimates in US dollars. The snapshot records a 7-day TVL change only, so no 30-day change is shown.
