TOKEN LAUNCH RADAR

Protocols that record no token yet, ranked by what a launch would be worth — and how to farm each one before it happens.

How the value model works

  1. Peer multiples by category. Every protocol that already trades is grouped by category; its median market-cap-to-TVL multiple, shrunk toward the global median, prices a pre-token protocol of the same kind.
  2. A size curve from the fit. A log-log regression of market cap on TVL over all tokenised peers gives a second, size-only estimate; the implied value blends the two 50/50.
  3. Momentum and track record. Seven-day TVL growth moves the estimate by up to ±25%, and each year a protocol has run with no token adds 6%, capped at four years.

Methodology. The radar reads one sealed DefiLlama snapshot and fits its model on the snapshot's own tokenised protocols: an ordinary-least-squares line in log10 space (log10 market cap against log10 TVL) and, per category, the median market-cap-to-TVL multiple shrunk toward the global median with weight n/(n+6). A pre-token protocol's base value is the geometric blend of the size curve and its category multiple applied to its own TVL; seven-day TVL momentum then scales it by 1 + 0.01 × the change clamped to ±25%, and its track record scales it by 1 + 6% per year tracked, capped at four years, with protocols younger than a quarter year discounted 10% instead. The stated range is the estimate times 10±0.45. The board covers the snapshot's full 40-protocol watchlist and fills the remaining rows with the largest pre-token protocols below the $50M floor that pass the watchlist's own hygiene: no bridge, liquid-staking, restaking or exchange categories, no wrapped or staked products, no versioned pool names, no exchange-named products. Farm plans are generated per category from each protocol's cheapest chain and TVL tier; costs are estimates in US dollars.
